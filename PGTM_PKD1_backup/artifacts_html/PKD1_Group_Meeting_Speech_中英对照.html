Slide 4

Main points of the paper文章主要内容

First, a short introduction to the paper. It is by Weisburd and colleagues, published this year in AJHG. The title is “Defining a tandem repeat catalog and variation clusters for genome-wide analyses and population databases”.

The problem they found is this. To study tandem repeats (TRs) across the whole genome, you first need a catalog of sites: where the repeats are and where their edges are. But the existing catalogs use different definitions and different standards, and they may miss polymorphic STRs. The paper did three things, shown by the three numbers on the slide.

First, they compared more than 10 existing catalogs.

Second, because of these differences, they merged four sources into one new catalog with 4.86 million sites, covering 2.1% of the reference genome. They also built the TRExplorer portal (https://trexplorer.broadinstitute.org/). On the web, you can look up information for each site, and you can filter and export it. (You can click the link to show it live.)

Third, they defined the idea of a “variation cluster”: a TR may not be isolated. It may sit inside a region of polymorphic sequence.

For me, three ideas in this paper are very useful: population allele frequency, variation cluster, and mappability. So I added all three to my screening script. On the next slide I will show where each one is used. Then I will show, in order: where the database comes from, variation clusters, and mappability.

先简单介绍一下这篇文章。它是 Weisburd 等人今年 5 月发表在 AJHG 上的，题目是《Defining a tandem repeat catalog and variation clusters for genome-wide analyses and population databases》。

它发现的问题是：做串联重复序列（TR）的全基因组分析，要先有一份“位点目录”，说明哪些位置是重复、边界在哪里。但现有的目录定义不同、标准不一致，也可能漏掉多态 STR。文章针对这个问题做了三件事，对应页面上的三个数字。

第一，比较了现有的 10 多个目录。

第二，针对这些差异，把四个来源合并成一个新目录，并做成了 TRExplorer 门户，可以直接在网页上查到每个位点的信息，也能筛选导出。（现场可以点开链接展示一下。）

第三，提出了“变异簇”的概念：一个 TR 可能不是孤立的，而是嵌在一片多态序列里。

对我来说，这里面有三个概念非常有用：人群等位基因频率、变异簇和可比对性，所以我把它们都加入了筛选脚本。下一页先看它们分别用在脚本的哪里，然后依次展示：数据库的来源、变异簇，以及可比对性。

Slide 5

How it links to our script与我们脚本的关系

This slide answers one question: how does the content of the paper get into my script? Each of the three ideas has one use in the script. Population allele frequency is used to calculate He and Ho and to rank sites into tiers. Variation cluster is used to flag VC and reject-VC, and to set the primer no-go zone, which is 25 bp on each side of the cluster. 这一页回答“文章的内容怎样进入我的脚本”，三个概念各对应脚本里的一个用法：人群等位基因频率，用来算 He、Ho 并分级；变异簇，用来标 VC 和拒绝-VC，并划定引物禁区，即簇区间两侧各 25 bp；可比对性低于 0.5 的位点，标“需 BLAST”，留到引物设计阶段处理。

Slide 7

Key idea: variation cluster (VC)变异簇（VC）的定义

This slide is about the definition of a variation cluster. A TR, together with the nearby sequence that is also highly variable in the population, is treated as one unit. Look at the picture. The top is an isolated TR: its flanks match the reference genome. The bottom is a variation cluster: the TR sits inside a region of polymorphic sequence, and the TRs and indels in the cluster change together. This whole interval is one variation cluster.

How is it decided? They use 100 HPRC HiFi samples and calculate, base by base, the chance of being part of a variation cluster. Starting from the TR, they extend 1 bp at a time on both sides, and stop when the chance falls below 0.5. It counts as a variation cluster only if the edge extends more than 5 bp. Overlapping clusters are merged. A cluster with 5 or more TRs is called a complex variation cluster, and there are 24,867 of them. The tiers in my script are based on these numbers.

变异簇的定义，对应这一页：一个 TR 连同它周围同样在人群中高度变异的序列，作为一个整体。看图，上面是孤立的 TR，两侧序列和参考基因组一致；下面是变异簇，TR 嵌在一片多态序列里，簇内相邻的 TR 和插入缺失会一起变化，这片区间就是一个变异簇。

判定方法：用 100 个 HPRC HiFi 样本，逐个碱基计算“属于变异簇的概率”；从 TR 往两侧每次延伸 1 bp，概率降到 0.5 以下就停止。边界外扩超过 5 bp 才记为变异簇，重叠的合并。含 5 个或以上 TR 的称为复杂变异簇，一共 24,867 个。后面脚本的分档，就以这些指标为基础。

Slide 8

Why variation clusters matter变异簇的作用

This slide shows why we should care about variation clusters. In HG002, the share of alleles that agree with the assembly is 96.21% for isolated TRs. For TRs inside a cluster that are called one by one, it is only 79.43%. If the whole cluster is called together, it is 90.58%. So sites inside a cluster are the main source of genotyping errors. This is also true for our markers that we genotype by CE. I will explain how the script handles this on slide 13.

这一页说明为什么要关注 VC：在 HG002 上，孤立 TR 和组装一致的等位基因比例是 96.21%，簇内 TR 逐个分型只有 79.43%，整簇一起分型是 90.58%。所以簇内位点是分型出错的主要来源，对我们用 CE 做分型的标记也一样。第 13 页会讲脚本怎样处理。

Slide 9

Key idea: mappability重要概念：可比对性（mappability）

The last idea is mappability. I have not talked about it on its own yet. It is an annotation that the catalog gives to every site, and it is calculated for the site and its two flanks. The lower the value, the harder it is to place the sequence reliably with short reads, so the harder it is to genotype. The portal notes say the same thing. The exact method is in Table S3 of the paper, and I will not go into it here.

The paper has a related finding: TRs inside clusters have lower mappability, and TRs with high mappability are almost all isolated TRs. So mappability and variation clusters are two connected views of the same problem.

In my script I use it like this. If mappability is below 0.5, the site gets the flag “needs BLAST”. This threshold is provisional. It is only a flag, not a removal. Tier A needs mappability of at least 0.5, so a site below 0.5 can only reach tier B. In the primer design priority, it also counts as 1 problem. Most important: for flagged sites, we must check specificity carefully at the primer design step. I will talk about that in Part 2.

Two PKD1 examples. Site 1756, an AC repeat, has a mappability of only 0.23, and I could not design primers for it in Part 2. Site 2960, an AACC repeat, is even lower, at 0.15, but it still passed the design, and it is now one of the 18 sites to test. So low mappability is a warning sign. It does not always mean that the design will fail.

Now that we have the three ideas, let’s look at how they changed the script, and how it differs from the old SOP.

最后一个概念是可比对性，前面还没有单独讲过。它是目录给每个位点附带的一项注释，针对位点及其两侧侧翼序列计算。数值越低，说明这段序列越难被短读长可靠地定位，也就越难准确分型，门户的说明里也是这样提醒的。具体的计算方法在文章的补充表 Table S3 里，我这里不展开。

文章里有一个相关的发现：簇内的 TR 可比对性偏低，而可比对性高的 TR 几乎都是孤立 TR。所以它和变异簇是两个相互关联的角度。

在我的脚本里是这样用的：可比对性低于 0.5，就标“需 BLAST”，这个阈值是暂定的。它只标记、不单独剔除；A 级要求可比对性不低于 0.5，低于 0.5 的位点最高只能是 B 级；在引物设计的优先序里，它也算 1 个问题。更重要的是，带这个标记的位点，到引物设计阶段要重点核对特异性，这一点在第二部分会讲。

举两个 PKD1 的例子：1756（AC 重复）可比对性只有 0.23，在第二部分没能设计出引物；2960（AACC 重复）可比对性更低，是 0.15，但仍然通过了设计，现在在 18 个验证位点里。所以可比对性低是风险提示，不等于一定设计不出引物。

Slide 11

Improvement 1: add population diversity改进一：加入人群多态性

I use the HPRC256 allele distribution to calculate He, which is 1 minus the sum of p squared. Then I put the sites into tiers by He. Tier A means He of at least 0.70, no VC, and mappability of at least 0.5; there are 6. Tier B has 81. Tier C means He below 0.60; there are 2,507. Excluded sites are 218: these are reject-VC or known disease sites. All these rules are provisional.

The chart on the right puts the 97 markers of the old SOP into the new tiers: A 5, B 28, C 59, excluded 5. So 64 markers, which is 66%, would be moved down or removed, mainly because their population diversity is low.

This is a kind of confidence game. Before, I could only test diversity by CE, with 16 samples. Now I can predict it, and remove STRs with low diversity at the very start.

One limit, or one direction to improve: the He now comes from multi-population data of 256 people. But the website lets me go further. For example, I can pick the 43 samples from East Asia. If we know that the patient is from East Asia, we could use that. The sample is small, though, so it may not be a reliable reference.

Slide 13

Improvement 2: use variation clusters改进二：考虑变异簇

This links back to slide 8. The script puts variation clusters into two levels. For me, a VC is something to consider, not a screen. There are two cases: one is direct rejection, the other is a VC warning. “Reject-VC” means 5 or more TRs in the cluster, or the cluster extends 50 bp or more beyond the site. These sites are excluded directly. For the “VC” warning, the other sites with a variation cluster are kept, but they drop from tier A to tier B, so their priority goes down.

Segmental duplications (UCSC genomicSuperDups, overlap of 20 bp or more) hit 8 sites. Common SNPs (snp151Common, 4 or more on either side) hit 21 sites. Key step 1: Primer3 design settings关键步骤一：设计与标记

This slide is about the Primer3 settings, which means the settings for the gene-specific part of the primer (the anchor), and why I set them this way.

First, Tm. Our annealing temperature in the lab is 60 ℃, but here I set the anchor Tm to 62–64 ℃, best 63 ℃. The reason is a rule of thumb: the annealing temperature is usually a few degrees below the primer Tm. IDT suggests 2 to 5 ℃ lower. This way the primer binds firmly, but does not bind too easily to wrong places. So to anneal at 60 ℃, I put the primer Tm in the window of 62 to 64 ℃. I must say, this is a rule of thumb, not something we have tested. The Tm value also depends on the calculation method and the buffer. I use the SantaLucia nearest-neighbor method, with 50 mM sodium, 1.5 mM magnesium, 0.6 mM dNTP and 50 nM primer. Later, the gradient gel from 55 to 65 ℃ will show if this window is good.

Second, length: 19 to 28 nt, best 24. Length is tied to Tm and GC. With this salt condition, I calculated with random sequences. To reach Tm 62–64 ℃, a sequence with 40% GC needs about 28 to 29 nt, 50% GC needs about 23 to 25 nt, and 60% GC needs about 20 to 21 nt. This is the small table on the right. So the lower limit of 19 nt fits high-GC sequences. The upper limit of 28 nt lets us reach the Tm in AT-rich flanks with 40% GC. Longer primers form more secondary structure, bind more to wrong places, and cost more. So I stop at 28. I must say, these limits are my own choice, not a fixed value from a paper.

Third, GC is 40–60%, which is a normal range, and I want a G or C at the 3′ end, so the extending end binds more firmly. The product is 100 to 250 bp, because single-cell WGA gives short template fragments, and short products amplify more easily. The product of the largest allele must be no more than 330 bp. If no candidate is found, there are four levels that relax Tm, product size and GC step by step, and they are marked in the order list.

All primers in this batch use one FAM tail, to remove the dye effect. Thermo says FAM signal is stronger than VIC, NED and PET. If we did not use one tail, we could not tell if a primer is bad or just the dye is weak.

这一页讲 Primer3 的设计条件，也就是引物基因特异部分（锚点）的参数，以及为什么这样设。

先说 Tm。我们实验的退火温度是 60 ℃，而这里把锚点的 Tm 设成 62–64 ℃，最适 63 ℃。原因是一个经验规则：退火温度一般比引物的 Tm 低几度，IDT 的建议是低 2 到 5 ℃，这样引物既能稳定结合，又不至于太容易非特异结合。所以要在 60 ℃ 退火，引物的 Tm 就放在 62 到 64 ℃ 这个窗口。要说明，这是经验规则，不是我们实验验证过的；Tm 的数值还依赖计算方法和缓冲液条件，我用的是 SantaLucia 最近邻，50 mM 钠离子、1.5 mM 镁离子、0.6 mM dNTP、50 nM 引物。后面做 55 到 65 ℃ 的梯度胶，会用结果检验这个窗口是否合适。

再说长度 19 到 28 nt，最适 24。长度和 Tm、GC 是绑在一起的。在这个盐条件下，我用随机序列算过：要让 Tm 到 62–64 ℃，GC 40% 的序列需要约 28 到 29 nt，GC 50% 约 23 到 25 nt，GC 60% 约 20 到 21 nt，就是页面右边这张小表。所以下限 19 nt 对应 GC 偏高的序列，上限 28 nt 是为了够得着 GC 40% 的 AT 富集侧翼。再长的引物更容易形成二级结构和非特异结合，合成也更贵，所以我把上限卡在 28。要说明，这个上下限是我的取舍，不是文献里的固定值。

GC 设 40–60%，是常规范围，3′端要有 G 或 C，让延伸端结合更稳。产物 100 到 250 bp，因为单细胞 WGA 的模板片段短，产物短更容易扩增；含最大等位基因不超过 330 bp。找不到候选时，会分四级逐级放宽 Tm、产物和 GC，并在订购表里标注。

这一批全部统一用 FAM 尾，原因是排除染料差异：Thermo 的资料给出 FAM 信号强于 VIC、NED 和 PET，不统一的话，没法区分是引物差还是染料弱。

Slide 22

Key step 2: three hard filters关键步骤二：三项硬性过滤

This slide is the core of the new work in this part: three hard filters. First, why these three and not others?

In our use, a primer pair can fail in three main ways. First, the template has variation. For PGT-M with single cells, a common SNP at the primer site can make one allele not amplify. This is allele dropout, and it directly causes mistakes in the linkage analysis. Second, primers bind each other and form dimers. This uses up primers, and in multiplex they also disturb each other. Third, the primer can also bind to other places in the genome and amplify other bands. PKD1 itself has 6 pseudogene copies, and this was the main suspect for the non-specific results in batch 2.

These three are exactly what Primer3 cannot cover alone. Primer3 can check Tm, GC, hairpins, and dimers inside a primer or between a pair. But it does not know where the SNPs are on the template. It does not search the whole genome to see where else the primer can bind. And we ask it to design the anchors without tails, so it cannot see the dimers after the tails are added. So these three filters fill these gaps. If a candidate pair fails any one of them, it is removed.

Filter 1: common SNP. The data come from UCSC snp151Common, with a population frequency of at least 1%. The rule is: no SNP within 5 bases of the 3′ end, because a 3′ end mismatch is the easiest way to stop the extension; and at most 1 SNP in the whole primer. This filter is mainly for a reason of principle: PGT-M uses single cells, and dropout costs a lot. The back-test of 24 pairs is not enough to test this one alone.

Filter 2: tailed primer-dimer. I use the thermodynamics of primer3-py. I add the FAM tail and the pigtail and calculate the whole sequence, because the primers in the real reaction have tails. There are two rules. If the heterodimer ΔG is −7 kcal/mol or lower, or the 3′ end stability is −4 kcal/mol or lower, the pair is removed. ΔG is free energy; the more negative, the more stable the binding. I look only at the 3′ end, because only dimers that can be extended at the 3′ end really affect amplification. This idea comes from PrimerROC. These two thresholds are provisional. They come from the back-test of 24 old pairs, which I explain below.

Filter 3: genome-wide specificity. I use my own k-mer engine. It takes the 3′ end of the primer as a seed and looks for the places where the primer may bind in the whole hg38. A “near-perfect copy” is defined like this: the 3′ end 12 nt match exactly, and the whole primer has at most 3 mismatches. There are three rules. If there are more than 20 other near-perfect copies, the pair is removed. The target product must be unique, which means there must be no other product made by any forward and reverse primers, besides the designed one. And if one primer makes more than 200 self-products with itself, it is also removed. The last two rules were added in v2, after the check.

Last, what does “24-pair back-test” mean? A back-test means that we use old primers with known lab results to test the new rules, looking backwards. These 24 pairs are primers that we really used before: 13 pairs from batch 1, all usable in CE, and 11 pairs from batch 2, tested by gradient gel, some good and some bad. I put these 24 pairs into the new engine and calculated, for each pair, the dimer values, the number of other near-perfect copies, and so on. Then I compared them with the lab results of that time, to see which number can separate the good from the bad.

The dimer thresholds were set this way. With −7 and −4 as limits, only two pairs, 3325 and 3353, are flagged, and both were non-specific in the lab. Among the usable primers, the worst values were only −6.5 and −3.4, so they are all outside the thresholds. The threshold for other near-perfect copies is set in the same way: usable primers have at most 14, and the bad primers have at least one primer with 63 or more. So I put the line at 20.

I must stress: 24 pairs is a small sample and there are exceptions, so all thresholds are only provisional. The details are on slide 27. The wet-lab results in Part 3 will be used to calibrate them.

这一页是这一部分新增的核心：三项硬性过滤。先说为什么是这三项，而不是别的。

一个引物对，在我们这个用途里，最主要的失败方式有三种。第一，模板本身有变异：单细胞 WGA 做 PGT-M，引物位点上的常见 SNP 会让某个等位基因扩不出来，也就是等位基因脱扣，会直接导致连锁分析出错。第二，引物之间互相结合：形成二聚体，既消耗引物，也会在 multiplex 里互相干扰。第三，引物在基因组的其他位置也能结合：会扩出别的条带，PKD1 本身还有 6 个假基因拷贝，这是批次 2 里非特异的主要嫌疑。

这三项正好是 Primer3 自己覆盖不了的部分：Primer3 能检查 Tm、GC、发夹以及引物自身和一对引物之间的二聚体，但它不知道模板上哪里有 SNP，也不会在整个基因组里查引物还能结合到哪里，而且我们让它设计的是不带尾的锚点，所以带尾后的二聚体它也看不到。所以我补的就是这三个缺口。每个候选引物对，任何一项不通过就被剔除。

第一项，常见 SNP。数据来自 UCSC 的 snp151Common，人群频率不低于 1% 的位点。规则是：引物 3′端 5 个碱基内不允许有，因为 3′端错配最容易让延伸失败；整条引物内最多 1 个。这一项主要是出于原理：PGT-M 是单细胞，脱扣的代价很大。回测的 24 对引物不足以单独检验这一项。

第二项，带尾二聚体。用 primer3-py 的热力学计算，把 FAM 尾和 pigtail 都加上，整条序列一起算，因为实际反应里存在的就是带尾的引物。判据有两个：异源二聚体 ΔG 不高于 −7 kcal/mol，或者 3′端稳定性不高于 −4 kcal/mol，就剔除。ΔG 是自由能，越负代表结合越稳定。只看 3′端，是因为只有 3′端能被延伸的二聚体才真正影响扩增，这个原则来自 PrimerROC。这两个阈值是暂定的，来自 24 对旧引物的回测，下面解释。

第三项，全基因组特异性。用我自己写的 k-mer 引擎，把引物 3′端当种子，在整个 hg38 里找它可能结合的位置。其中“近完全拷贝”定义为：3′端 12 nt 完全匹配，并且全长错配不超过 3 个。判据有三条：其他近完全拷贝超过 20 个就剔除；目标产物必须唯一，也就是除了设计的那个产物，不能再有别的正反引物配成的产物；同一条引物自己和自己形成的产物超过 200 个也剔除，这两条是 v2 在复核之后新增的。

最后解释“24 对回测”是什么意思。回测，就是用已经有实验结果的旧引物，反过来检验新规则。这 24 对是我们之前实际做过的引物：批次 1 的 13 对，CE 都能用；批次 2 的 11 对，做了梯度胶，有好有坏。我把这 24 对放进新引擎，算出每一对的二聚体、其他近完全拷贝数等指标，再和它们当时的实验结果对照，看哪个指标能把好的和差的分开。

二聚体的阈值就是这样定的：用 −7 和 −4 作为界限，只有 3325 和 3353 两对被标出来，而这两对在实验里都是非特异；可用的引物里，最差也只有 −6.5 和 −3.4，都在阈值之外。其他近完全拷贝数的阈值也是这样：可用的引物不超过 14 个，差的引物至少有一条在 63 个以上，所以把线放在 20。

要强调的是：24 对样本很小，而且有例外，所以所有阈值都只是暂定，细节在后面第 27 页。第三部分的湿实验结果，就是用来校准它们的。

Slide 25

Tools we checked: function, what we took, and trade-offs评估过的工具：功能、借鉴点和取舍

This slide answers three questions: what is each tool for, what did I take from it, and why did I not use it directly.

MFEprimer is a tool for checking the quality of primers. It uses a k-mer index to find all the places where a primer may bind in the genome. It predicts which products will be made, including off-target products, and it calculates the binding energy, dimers and hairpins. It is the main reference for my specificity check. I did not use its program version, because the security software on my computer blocks it, and I did not try to get around that. Only the web server is possible, but it is limited to 500 primers, and I cannot connect it to my workflow to run by itself. So I took its idea of the 3′ seed and wrote my own engine, and I used the web server for an independent check. After the final primers are designed, I also plan to keep using the web server to check them.

AutoDimer is a program from NIST. It screens a multiplex primer set for primer-dimers and hairpins. It was first made for forensic STR multiplex systems. This is exactly the function we need later for the multiplex panel, so I want to use it. NIST now recommends the web version. I have checked that the web version opens, and it is on the NIST DNA Analysis Tools site. The limit is that I must upload a sequence file by hand, with up to 100 sequences and up to 50 nucleotides each. Our tailed primers are a little over 40 nt, so it is fine, but it cannot join my workflow automatically. The Visual Basic program version is no longer supported.

PrimerROC predicts primer-dimers and sets the threshold with ROC analysis. It gave me one principle: only dimers that can be extended at the 3′ end really affect amplification. But its threshold of −4.43 fits its own score and its own lab conditions, so I cannot copy it to our system. That is why I set my thresholds with the back-test of 24 old pairs. SADDLE uses simulated annealing to pick, from many candidates, a multiplex primer set with the fewest dimers. I used this idea in my panel step. But its method needs a non-disclosure agreement and is patent pending, so I only took the idea and wrote the program myself.

openPrimeR checks if a multiplex primer set can amplify each template. Array Melt measures DNA hairpin thermodynamics at high throughput and gives more accurate ΔG parameters. I did not use these two. openPrimeR is made for IGHV, Taq and gels. It has not been tested on STR or single-cell WGA, and it does not predict peak height. The lab conditions of Array Melt are 25 mM NaCl without magnesium, and its dimer ΔG has not been validated. LUS is a factor for choosing STRs, not a way to adjust primer design. I will think about it later in STR screening.

这一页回答三件事：这些工具各自是做什么的，我借鉴了什么，为什么没有直接用。

MFEprimer 的功能是引物质量检查：用 k-mer 索引在基因组里找出引物可能结合的所有位置，预测会扩出哪些产物，包括非目标产物，并计算结合的热力学、二聚体和发夹。它是我这套特异性检查的主要参考。我没有直接用它的程序版，因为程序版在本机被安全软件拦截，我没有去绕过；能用的只有网页版，但网页版限 500 条引物，也没法接到我的流程里自动运行。所以我借鉴它的 3′端种子思路自己写了引擎，再用网页版做独立复核。后面设计出最终引物之后，我也打算继续用网页版复核。

AutoDimer 是 NIST 做的程序，专门筛查多重引物组里的引物二聚体和发夹，原本是为法医 STR 多重体系设计的。这正好是我们后面做多重面板需要的功能，所以我想采用。NIST 现在推荐用网页版，我已经确认网页版可以打开，入口在 NIST 的 DNA Analysis Tools 网站。它的限制是要手动上传序列文件，最多 100 条序列，每条最多 50 个核苷酸；我们带尾的引物长度 40 多 nt，没有超限，但不能自动接到我的流程里。Visual Basic 的程序版已经不再维护。

PrimerROC 的功能是预测引物二聚体，并用 ROC 分析来校准判定阈值，它给了我一个原则：只有 3′端能被延伸的二聚体才真正影响扩增。但它的阈值 −4.43 是针对它自己的评分和实验条件的，不能直接搬到我们的体系，所以阈值我用 24 对旧引物回测来定。SADDLE 的功能是用模拟退火，从大量候选里选出二聚体最少的多重 PCR 引物组，这个思路用在了我的面板步骤；但它的算法需要保密协议、已申请专利，所以只借用思路，程序自己写。

openPrimeR 评估多重引物组能否扩增各个模板，Array Melt 是高通量测量 DNA 发夹热力学、给出更准确的 ΔG 参数。这两个评估后没有采用：openPrimeR 针对的是 IGHV、Taq 和凝胶，没有在 STR 或单细胞 WGA 上验证，也不预测峰高；Array Melt 的实验条件是 25 mM NaCl、不含镁离子，它的二聚体 ΔG 没有被验证过。LUS 是 STR 选择的因素，不是引物设计的手段，留到后续的 STR 筛选里考虑。

Slide 26

Checking the engine: independent tests引擎验证：独立复核

I wrote the engine myself, so I must show that its output can be trusted. I will explain this step in detail. I did three checks, from simple to complex. First I check the logic on artificial data. Then I check the real hard case, the PKD1 pseudogenes, to see if the engine can find them. Last, I check with an outside tool.

Check 1, nine unit tests. I build a small artificial genome and put in sites where I already know the answer. Then I check if the output of the engine is right. Can it find exact matches? Can it find sites with mismatches and record where the mismatches are? Does it treat a mismatch at the 3′ end correctly? Can it find sites on the reverse strand? Is the binding energy weaker when there are more mismatches? Can it pair the forward and reverse sites into a product correctly? All nine tests passed. This check only shows that the code logic is not wrong. It does not show that the engine works well in the real genome.

Check 2, the PKD1 pseudogene control. This is for the hard case that we care about most. The 5′ part of PKD1 has a repeat region, at about 2.10 to 2.12 Mb on chr16. On chr16 it has 6 pseudogene copies with very similar sequence. In this repeat region I picked 40 random primer pairs. Each primer is 22 nt, with 40–60% GC and a G or C at the 3′ end, and the product is 150 to 450 bp. These primers bind PKD1, and they should also have matching binding sites in the 6 pseudogene copies. I let the engine scan the whole hg38, to see if it can find, in each copy, one forward site and one reverse site that make a product. The product must be within 3 kb of the copy region, and its size must be within 300 bp of the target. We cannot ask for exactly the same size, because the tandem repeats have different lengths in each copy. There are 40 pairs times 6 copies, which is 240 cases in total, and the engine found 194. For the 46 that it did not find, I compared them with the reference by hand, one by one. 40 are truly not there: the copy has no similar sequence in this region, or the best match has many mismatches, or the 3′ end does not match. The other 6 are probably real misses, which means the engine can miss some sites in rare cases.

Check 3, an independent check with the MFEprimer web server. I took the anchor sequences of 21 old pairs and 45 new pairs, 66 pairs in total. These are the gene-specific parts without the tail. I submitted them to the web server, with hg38 as the reference, and compared the target product size that it listed with my engine and with the design. All 66 pairs agreed. I must say, this only checks the target product. For off-target products, the two tools disagree on a few primers. For the old pairs 3325 and 3353, my engine flagged off-target binding, but the web server did not list any. The sample is too small to say who is right.

The check also found that the pairs 1876 and 1275 in v1 had a multi-product problem. So I added two rules, “target product must be unique” and “self-product limit”, and made v2 again. This is the change from v1 to v2.

自己写的引擎，需要证明它的输出可信。这一步我详细说一下，做了三项检查，思路是由简到繁：先在人造数据上验证逻辑，再在真实的难点——PKD1 假基因上看能不能找出来，最后用外部工具独立核对。

第一项，9 项单元测试。做法是在人工合成的小基因组里，埋入我已经知道答案的位点，然后检查引擎的输出对不对：能不能找到完全匹配的位点，能不能找到有错配的位点并记下错配位置，3′末端错配该不该排除，反向链上的位点能不能找到，错配越多结合的热力学是不是越弱，找到的正反位点能不能正确配成扩增产物。9 项全部通过。这一项只能说明代码逻辑没写错，不能说明在真实基因组里好用。

第二项，PKD1 假基因对照，这是针对我们最关心的难点。PKD1 的 5′部分有一段重复区，大约在 chr16 的 2.10 到 2.12 Mb，在 chr16 上有 6 个假基因拷贝，序列非常相似。我在这个重复区里随机取了 40 对引物，每条 22 nt，GC 在 40–60%，3′端是 G 或 C，产物在 150 到 450 bp。这些引物在 PKD1 上有，在 6 个假基因拷贝上也应该有对应的结合位置。我让引擎对整个 hg38 扫描，看它能不能在每个拷贝上，找到一个正向位点加一个反向位点配成的产物，产物落在拷贝区域前后 3 kb 内，大小和目标相差不超过 300 bp——因为各个拷贝的串联重复长度不一样，所以不能要求大小完全相等。40 对引物乘 6 个拷贝，一共 240 个组合，引擎找到了 194 个。没找到的 46 个，我逐个和参考序列直接比对：40 个是真的没有——那个拷贝在这个区域没有同源序列，或者最佳比对错配很多、3′端不匹配；剩下 6 个是疑似真漏检，说明引擎在个别情况下会漏掉一些位点。

第三项，用 MFEprimer 网页版独立复核。做法是把旧引物 21 对、新设计 45 对，共 66 对的锚点序列，也就是不含尾的基因特异部分，提交到网页版，参考库选 hg38，比较它列出的目标产物大小，和我的引擎、和设计值是否一致。结果 66 对全部一致。要说明，这只是核对了目标产物；对非目标产物，两个工具在个别引物上有分歧：旧引物 3325 和 3353，我的引擎标出了非目标结合，网页版没有列出，样本太小，没法判断谁对。
