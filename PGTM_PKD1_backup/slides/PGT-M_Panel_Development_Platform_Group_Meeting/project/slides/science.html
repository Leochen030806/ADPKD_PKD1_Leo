NOT ONLY AN ENGINEERING GAIN

Feeding measured data back into scoring

Today

Scoring is only a prediction

Candidates are scored and that is the end of it. CE-observed heterozygosity never flows back, and the weights are unvalidated.

With the platform

Predicted score meets observed HET

Each marker's predicted score, observed heterozygosity and failure mode are stored together as an analysable dataset.

After two or three genes we have a scoring model that can be validated, even fitted — "how well our score predicts observed heterozygosity" is a concrete, quantifiable result.

The PKD2 work already produced a revised scoring system; the platform turns it into a configurable scorer rather than reinventing it.
