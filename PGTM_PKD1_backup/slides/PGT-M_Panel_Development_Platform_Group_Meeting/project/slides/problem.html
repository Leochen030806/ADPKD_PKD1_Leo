THE PROBLEM

What the current way of working costs us

Knowledge lives in one head

The whole PKD1 process is spread across memory, desktop spreadsheets, slide decks and screenshot folders. Nothing answers "where are we now".

Manual, and not reproducible

UCSC, NCBI and IDT are all clicked through by hand. The same gene run twice is not guaranteed to give the same answer.

A new gene means starting over

PKD2 and BRCA1 cannot inherit the PKD1 workflow or its criteria. Every judgement has to be passed on by a person again.

Decisions cannot be traced

Why a marker was dropped, which multiplex sets were already tried — all of it sits in memory. This is clinical-adjacent work.
