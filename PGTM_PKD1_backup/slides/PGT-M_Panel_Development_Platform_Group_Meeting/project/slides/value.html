VALUE TO THE LAB

What we get once it exists

01

Much shorter cycle per gene

Dry-lab work drops from days of browser clicking to minutes of scripted runs; people step in only at the decision points.

02

The workflow becomes an SOP

It stops depending on one person's memory and habits. What gets handed over is a pipeline that runs, not a pile of folders.

03

Data in one place

Any marker's full history is one click away — when it was designed, when ordered, every result, and why it was kept or dropped.

04

Traceable end to end

This is clinical-adjacent work. "Why was it decided that way" has to stay answerable, and memory plus scattered files cannot do that.
