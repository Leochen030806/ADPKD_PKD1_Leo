The host group

Where our work meets theirs

Both groups work on the same clinical pathway, at opposite ends of it.

Step 1

Carrier screening and diagnosis

Identify couples or patients at risk of a monogenic disorder.

ZURICH · Praeclare
Step 2

Variant classification

Decide whether the familial variant is pathogenic. PGT-M is only offered once this is settled.

ZURICH · SwissGenVar
Step 3

Linkage panel design and family work-up

Choose informative STR markers around the gene, design and validate the panel, haplotype the family.

NUS · Chong lab
Step 4

Embryo testing

Direct mutation detection plus multi-marker haplotyping on embryo biopsies.

NUS · PGD Centre, NUH

The visit would join steps 2 and 3. A variant classified in Zurich could feed directly into an automatically designed linkage panel, and population data would tell us which markers are informative for a European rather than an Asian family.

The two-month project

From variant to embryo: linking variant interpretation and STR linkage-panel design for PGT-M, a PKD1/PKD2 pilot
Host
Prof. Anita Rauch, Institute of Medical Genetics, UZH
Funded period
1 May – 30 June 2027 (8 weeks)
Optional
Extension into July at my own cost, only if my NUS enrolment covers it
Objective 1
Measure the heterozygosity of candidate PKD1/PKD2 STR markers in European and East Asian populations using public datasets (for example 1000 Genomes and HPRC; coverage to be checked).
Objective 2
Prototype a module in PGT-M Panel Studio that takes a variant through evidence review (ACMG/AMP criteria, following the host's practice and SwissGenVar) to a PGT-M feasibility check and an automatically generated linkage-panel design.
Weeks 1–2

Learn the group's variant-interpretation workflow and the clinical pathway to PGT-M in Switzerland.

Weeks 3–5

Population informativeness analysis; report on European versus East Asian marker choice.

Milestone 1 · needs public data only
Weeks 6–8

Build the variant-to-panel prototype; pilot on public PKD1/PKD2 variants, and on de-identified institute cases if permitted.

Milestone 2
July (optional, self-funded)

Write-up, lab presentation, manuscript outline.

Deliverables
Population-specific marker report; working prototype with a pilot evaluation; lab presentation; manuscript outline
