### 6.3 1 页研究计划

**From variant to embryo: linking variant interpretation and STR linkage-panel design for PGT-M — a PKD1/PKD2 pilot**

Applicant: Xiaoyang (Leo) Chen, Department of Paediatrics, NUS (supervisor: Prof. Samuel S. Chong)
Proposed host: Prof. Anita Rauch, Institute of Medical Genetics, University of Zurich
Duration: May–June 2027 (8 weeks)

**Background.** PGT-M for ADPKD relies on STR haplotyping around PKD1/PKD2, because allele dropout makes direct mutation testing in single cells unreliable. Two steps decide whether a family can be offered PGT-M: firm classification of the familial variant, and a linkage panel that is informative in that family's population. These steps are usually handled separately.

**Objectives.**
1. Quantify the heterozygosity of candidate PKD1/PKD2 STR markers in European and East Asian populations using public datasets.
2. Prototype a module that takes a variant through evidence review (ACMG/AMP criteria, guided by the host group's practice and SwissGenVar) to a PGT-M feasibility check and an automatically generated linkage-panel design.

**Plan and milestones.**
- Weeks 1–2: Learn the host group's variant-interpretation workflow and the clinical pathway to PGT-M in Switzerland.
- Weeks 3–5: Population informativeness analysis; report on European versus East Asian marker selection. (Milestone 1)
- Weeks 6–8: Build the variant-to-panel prototype in PGT-M Panel Studio and pilot it on public PKD1/PKD2 variants, and on de-identified institute cases if permitted. (Milestone 2)

**Deliverables.** Population-specific marker report; working prototype with a pilot evaluation; lab presentation; manuscript outline.

**Data.** Public data first (e.g. 1000 Genomes, HPRC); institute data only under the host's approvals.

**Skills I will gain.** Clinical-grade variant interpretation; how reproductive genetic testing is delivered in a European setting.
